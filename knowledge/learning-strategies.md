# Effective learning strategies for technical skills

## Practise deliberately

Work on tasks slightly beyond your current level, get feedback quickly, and repeat. Reading or watching without practice tends to create a feeling of understanding without the ability to apply it.

## Retrieval and spacing

Test yourself instead of re-reading: solve a problem from memory, or explain a concept without notes. Revisit topics after increasing intervals (for example after one day, three days, then a week) rather than cramming them once.

## Build, then read

For practical skills, build something small first and then read the documentation to understand what you used. Questions that come from real use make the reading far more effective.

## Learn prerequisites first

Many skills depend on others. Machine learning depends on Python, statistics and some linear algebra; React depends on JavaScript, HTML and CSS. TwinPath's roadmap orders prerequisites first for this reason. Skipping them usually costs more time later.

## Plan around your real availability

Set a realistic number of weekly hours. A consistent 6 hours a week often beats an ambitious 20 hours that collapses after two weeks. You can update your weekly hours in TwinPath and regenerate your roadmap whenever your schedule changes.

## Track evidence, not just hours

Progress is shown by what you can do, not by how long you studied. After finishing a roadmap item, link the resulting work to your twin as evidence, or ask a mentor for a short assessment.

## Use resources critically

Prefer official documentation and well-maintained open materials. Check the date of tutorials, because tools change quickly. In TwinPath, links marked "unverified" could not be checked automatically, so confirm they still work before relying on them.
